EXPRESSION LAB - PHAN LOAI BIEU CAM KHUON MAT

1. Cai va mo Docker Desktop (Linux containers).
2. Can it nhat 3-5 GB trong tren o C cho lan build dau.
3. Nhan dup RUN.bat. Cho tai thu vien va build.
4. Trinh duyet mo http://localhost:8010
5. Dang ky tai khoan hoac dung tai khoan quan tri in trong cua so khoi dong.
6. Dong y xu ly hinh anh, chon anh/video/webcam va bat dau.
7. Nhan STOP.bat de dung, du lieu MongoDB van duoc giu.

Neu Docker bao read-only sau khi o C day: don dung luong va khoi dong lai Docker Desktop.
Neu cong 8010 ban: sua APP_PORT trong .env thanh 8011 roi chay lai.

Ban nay co model pretrained that de chay ngay.
Muon lam phan nghien cuu FER2013 / train 3 model: xem docs/NGHIEN_CUU.md.
Chua co ket qua accuracy / F1 nghien cuu khi chua train va test dataset that.

Xem README.md de biet huong dan chi tiet va docs/KIEM_THU.md de biet cac buoc da/chua xac minh.
